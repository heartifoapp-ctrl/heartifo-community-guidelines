Heartifo Community Guidelines

Last Updated: 18 September 2026

1. Respect Everyone

Treat customers, Captains and Heartifo support staff with respect. Harassment, threats, bullying, abusive behaviour or intimidation are not permitted.

2. No Discrimination

Heartifo does not permit discriminatory behaviour based on personal characteristics protected by applicable law.

3. Keep Rides Safe

Do not engage in dangerous, reckless or unlawful behaviour during a Heartifo ride. Follow applicable traffic and safety requirements.

4. No Illegal Activities

Heartifo must not be used for illegal activities, transportation of illegal goods or other unlawful purposes.

5. No Fraud or Misuse

Do not create fake accounts, provide false information, manipulate GPS/location information, abuse promotions or attempt to manipulate the Heartifo platform.

6. Respect Property

Customers and Captains must respect each other's personal property and the vehicle used for the ride. Deliberate damage or misuse is not permitted.

7. Privacy and Personal Information

Do not unnecessarily share, publish or misuse another person's personal information, phone number, location or other private data.

8. Ratings and Reviews

Ratings and reviews should be honest and based on the actual ride experience. Fake, abusive, threatening or misleading reviews are not permitted.

9. Prohibited Items

Do not carry illegal, dangerous or hazardous items through Heartifo. Applicable laws and transportation safety requirements must always be followed.

10. Reporting a Problem

If you experience harassment, unsafe behaviour, fraud or another serious issue, please contact Heartifo Support as soon as possible.

11. Enforcement

Heartifo may investigate reported violations and may restrict, suspend or terminate an account where appropriate and permitted by applicable law.
