Community Guidelines

Heartifo is built to create a safe, respectful and reliable experience for everyone using our platform.

Last Updated: 18 September 2026
01

Respect Everyone

Treat customers, Captains and Heartifo support staff with respect and courtesy. Harassment, threats, bullying, intimidation or abusive behaviour are not permitted.

02

Safety Comes First

Everyone using Heartifo should follow applicable safety and traffic requirements. Do not engage in dangerous, reckless or unlawful behaviour during a ride.

03

No Discrimination

Heartifo does not permit discriminatory behaviour or harassment based on personal characteristics protected under applicable law.

04

Honest Information

Keep your account and ride information accurate. Do not create fake accounts or provide false information to misuse Heartifo services.

05

No Fraud or Platform Abuse

Do not manipulate GPS information, abuse promotions, create duplicate accounts, misuse payment systems, or attempt to interfere with Heartifo's platform.

06

Respect Personal Property

Customers and Captains must respect each other's belongings and the vehicle used during a ride. Deliberate damage or misuse is not permitted.

07

Protect Personal Information

Do not unnecessarily share, publish or misuse another person's phone number, location, account information or other private data.

08

Ratings & Reviews

Ratings and reviews should be honest and based on the actual ride experience. Fake, abusive, threatening or misleading reviews are not permitted.

09

No Illegal Activities

Heartifo must not be used for illegal activities, transportation of illegal goods or any other unlawful purpose.

10

Prohibited Items

Do not carry illegal, dangerous or hazardous items through Heartifo. Applicable laws and transportation safety requirements must always be followed.

11

Report a Problem

If you experience harassment, unsafe behaviour, fraud or another serious issue, contact Heartifo Support as soon as possible so the matter can be reviewed.

12

Enforcement

Heartifo may investigate reported violations and may restrict, suspend or terminate an account where appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

Need Help?

If you have questions or need to report a serious issue, please contact the Heartifo Support team.
